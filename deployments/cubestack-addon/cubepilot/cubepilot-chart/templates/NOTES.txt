Notes:
- Platform CRDs are installed from the chart crds/ dir at first install.
  Helm does NOT upgrade CRDs; update them by reapplying the manifests in
  deploy/charts/cubepilot-chart/crds/ after a chart upgrade.
- The openclaw-config Secret is generated by the operator; the
  agent-kubeconfig Secret is rendered by this chart. scripts/setup.sh only
  seeds the cubepilot-llm Secret (platform default LLM apiKey) -- no host
  OpenClaw config is read.
